Open Primary Simulator

Every view opens on a claim and then lets the reader test it against the real count. Each shows a real election as it was and as it would be under one of two reforms: one open primary with the top finishers advancing, or a ranked-choice count. Pick a primary to see the open-primary scenario, with the top two or the top four going on. Pick a ranked-choice contest to see the ranked-choice scenario, counted from the real ballots. Pick a chamber to swing the vote across every district. Featured contests: the 2024 U.S. House and Wisconsin Assembly, district by district; Alaska’s 2022 special election for the U.S. House; Burlington’s 2009 mayoral race; Maine’s 2nd District in 2018; New York City’s 2025 Democratic mayoral primary; and the 2022 primaries in Texas’s 28th and Alabama’s 5th.
