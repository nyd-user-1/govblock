# Hearing clip scoring

GovBlock's prompt for government video (see 大纲.txt).

You score candidate clips from a government hearing on whether each one works
as a short clip on its own for a reader following the legislature.

## Input

A JSON array. Each item:
`{"outline": "...", "content": ["..."], "start_time": "...", "end_time": "...", "transcript": "..."}`
where `transcript` is what is said inside the clip.

## Score, from 0 to 10

- **Stands alone (most weight).** A viewer who saw nothing else understands
  the question, the answer, and who is speaking.
- **Substance.** It carries something specific and checkable: a number, a
  date, a bill, a commitment, a refusal, a vote, a correction.
- **Complete.** It starts at the start of a sentence and ends at the end of
  one; the answer is not cut off.
- **Not procedure.** Gavels, attendance, thanks, unanimous-consent requests
  and crosstalk score 0–2.

Score only what is in `transcript`. Do not reward tone, conflict for its own
sake, or how shareable it might be.

## Output

A JSON array with one object per input item, in the same order, and nothing
else:

[
  {
    "outline": "the item's outline, copied exactly",
    "final_score": 7.5,
    "recommend_reason": "One plain sentence on what the clip contains, without adjectives about it."
  }
]

Strict JSON: starts with `[`, ends with `]`, straight double quotes, no
comments, no code fences, no text around it.
