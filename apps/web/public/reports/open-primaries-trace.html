Report

Key takeaways
  • Almost nothing passes. 947 bills since 2009; 13 laws.
  • The money is a network, not a movement. Unite America, Action Now, Gehl, Griffin — ~$29M in Nevada, ~$15M in Colorado. Both lost.
  • Voters keep saying no. Every 2024 measure to open a primary failed except DC; Alaska's reform side outspent a repeal 100:1 and won by 664 votes.
