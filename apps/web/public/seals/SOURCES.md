# Seals — where each file came from

Harvested 2026-09-02 for the nominations list (`/docs/nominations`) and the CRS
reports list (`/docs/reports`). The organizations are the distinct
`organization` values in `congress_nominations`; each file below is the
Wikimedia Commons file named, rendered to 144 px wide (an avatar is 36 CSS px,
and several of the originals are megabyte SVGs).

Every file is public domain or CC0. Nothing here was cropped, recoloured or
redrawn — where a logo will not survive a circle it is drawn in a rectangular
avatar instead, which is what the `shape` column says.

## No usable seal on Commons — Senate seal fallback

Searched by name, by `Seal of the United States <name>`, by the `US-<Name>-Seal`
convention Commons uses for federal agencies, and by prefix. Nothing found that
is both the organization's own emblem and public domain:

- **African Development Bank**
- **European Bank for Reconstruction and Development**
- **Federal Agricultural Mortgage Corporation**
- **Foreign Service**
- **United States International Development Finance Corporation**
- **United States Postal Service**

Plus the 17 nominations whose `organization` is `None` — nothing to look up.

These render the Senate seal, which is the chamber the nomination is before —
the page says what we have rather than approximating an emblem we do not.

Two near-misses deliberately **not** used, because they are a different body:
the U.S. Postal *Inspection* Service seal for the Postal Service, and the Post
Office Department seal (1837–1970), which the Postal Service replaced in 1971.
