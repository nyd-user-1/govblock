# Congress.gov legislative-action taxonomy

The full set of "All Legislative Actions" filters from congress.gov/advanced-search.
Reference for auditing congress_bill_actions coverage and bill-status display.

## Introduction and Referral
Introduced in House; Introduced in Senate; with introductory remarks; introduced by request;
Referred to House committee; Referred to Senate committee; Referred to House subcommittee; Referred to Senate subcommittee.

## Committee-Related Activity
House committee/subcommittee actions (incl. hearings and markups); Senate committee/subcommittee actions (incl. hearings and markups);
House hearings; Senate hearings; House markups; Senate markups; House committee time extension; Senate committee time extension;
Reported/Committee discharged; Reported to House; Reported to Senate; House discharge petition filed; House committee discharged; Senate committee discharged.

## Floor Consideration
House floor actions; Senate floor actions; floor action that did not become law;
considered under suspension of rules in House (101st-) and its variants (before initial passage; passed; failed; failed then passed; other);
Passed/agreed to; same day in House and Senate; in House; in House w/ no Senate floor action; in House w/ no Senate committee or floor action;
in Senate; in Senate w/ no House floor action; in Senate w/ no House committee or floor action;
Failed of passage/not agreed to; in House; in Senate.

## Amendment Actions
House amendment actions (offered/reported by; offered; agreed to; not agreed to; other; agreed to by House; failed by House; other by House);
Senate amendment actions (submitted; referred to committee; proposed on the floor; agreed to; not agreed to; other).

## Roll Call Votes
All roll call votes on measures (excludes amendments); on measures in House; on measures in Senate; on amendments in House; on amendments in Senate.

## Resolving Differences
House actions; Senate actions; Conference committee actions; Conference report filed; agreed to in House; disagreed to in House; agreed to in Senate; disagreed to in Senate.

## To President
Presented to President; presented when latest action; Signed by President.

## Became Law
Public Laws; Private Laws; Public laws unsigned by the President; Public Law enacted over veto.

## Veto Consideration
Vetoes and Pocket Vetoes; Pocket vetoed; Vetoed by President; Passed House over veto; Passed Senate over veto; Failed in House over veto; Failed in Senate over veto.
